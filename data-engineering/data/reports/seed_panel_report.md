# Curated evolution seed panel

The panel contains **100** deterministic seeds selected from 1,214 validated training sequences with at least one local MIC observation. 831 sequences satisfy net charge pH 7.4 from +2 to +8 and Eisenberg hydrophobic moment >=0.2.

Final selection uses deterministic max-min farthest-point sampling in z-scored (charge, hydrophobic moment, length) space, yielding a 100-member spread rather than selecting only the lowest-MIC peptides.

`motif_library.csv` has 4,950 qualifying 4-8 residue motifs, each observed in at least three activity-supported sequences and containing a K/R plus W/F/L/I/V within four positions.

Natural origin is not asserted: available local provenance is not a reliable natural/synthetic classifier. The panel is activity-supported and diverse, not verified natural-only.
